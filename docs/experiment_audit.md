| Finding | Verified evidence | Treatment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Qwen3.5 curated Shot and Chain duplicate | 35 ordinary/Cyrillic files; 4665/4665 prediction pairs equal; legacy prompt builders identical | Do not report these as independent chain-gloss evidence. Generate 35 separate Qwen3.5 chain_gloss_v2 conditions. |

| Lezgi Qwen3.5 XCOMET missing | 12 original plus 10 Cyrillic files; each has 87 source/reference-matching rows | Score existing predictions only. Explicit target list: configs/lezgi_missing_xcomet.json. |
| Historical baseline language default Gitksan | Qwen, ModelGloss and Apertus default label did not reliably follow --language | Preserve historical metrics and disclose limitation. Future default label follows dataset language. |

Historical non-curated Qwen3.5 files are not all duplicates. The duplicate finding
must not be generalized to every old chain experiment. MTOB and API experiments
are not regenerated by this repair campaign.

## Resource Constraint

One H100 per job, FP32, no CPU offload, no quantization, unchanged images.
Historical Tsez OOM logs 5747097/5747098 show additional attention allocations
of about 44.5 GiB when only about 10 GiB remained. A successful static check
does not establish hardware feasibility. Preflight results are stored separately
under results/preflight and never used as full-test metrics.

## Historical Artifacts

Original filenames and exact old-to-new paths are recorded in the migration
manifests. Historical JSON/JSONL bodies are not rewritten to fake new provenance
or convert old Chain into explicit chain-gloss. Their embedded historical paths
may still refer to the old layout; use the migration manifest to resolve them.
The follow-up manifest records subsequent archival moves. Archive launchers are
not executable recommendations and may retain historical settings.
