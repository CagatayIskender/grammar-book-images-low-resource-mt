# Extraction audit

The five PDF extractions were manually sampled at the beginning, middle, end,
and throughout every configured Gl range. No existing summary, cheat sheet,
screenshot, or previous experiment output was consulted.

- Gitksan PDF 1: embedded text is readable; pages 2-10 contain the relevant IPA
  illustration body. Pages 11-12 are references and are excluded from Gl.
- Gitksan PDF 2: embedded text is present on all pages, but the source PDF itself
  contains visibly degraded character recognition and typographic noise. Pages
  1-7 are retained in Gl and bibliography pages 8-9 are excluded.
- Natugu: embedded text is readable across the grammar. Pages 2-68 retain the
  grammar body and abbreviation material; references are excluded from Gl.
- Lezgi: PDF pages 6 and 8 have no usable embedded text, but both are front
  matter outside Gl. Pages 19-35 and 48-59 contain the available abbreviations,
  grammar orientation, notation, and phonological description.
- Tsez: embedded text is readable on all pages. Pages 1-36 and abbreviation page
  40 are retained; bibliography pages 37-39 are excluded from Gl.

No OCR was required. Extracted text preserves URL text and Unicode. The only
automatic removal is repeated top/bottom marginal text, recorded per page and
in each extraction manifest.
